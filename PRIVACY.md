# Wick — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Wick is built to be private by design. There are no accounts, no logins, no
advertising, and no cross-app tracking. The only thing we count is a handful
of anonymous play events, described below.

## Single-player (Daily & Practice) — 100% on your device

- Your guesses, questions, stats, streaks, and coins are stored **only on your device**.
- Closeness ("warmth") scoring uses Apple's on-device Natural Language models.
- "Ask the Keeper" runs on-device using Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models).
- Nothing you type in single-player is sent to us or any third party.

## Play counts (since version 3.3)

To know whether the game is working, the app sends a few anonymous events:
that a daily puzzle was started, solved, or given up (with the puzzle number
and how many guesses it took), and that a share or race link was used. Each
event carries a random per-install id so we can count players; the server
hashes that id before storing it, and it is not tied to your name, email,
Apple ID, or contacts. No word, question, or anything you type is ever
included. These counts are used only to improve the game and are not shared
or sold.

## Live Race — the one place data leaves your device

Live multiplayer needs a server to pair players and score fairly, so when — and
only when — you play a live Race:

- The **words and questions you type** are sent to Wick's server to be scored
  (warmth) and answered (the Keeper).
- To compute warmth and answers, the server passes that text to **Google's
  Gemini API**. It is used solely to score the round; it is not used to train
  models on your identity, and it is not sold.
- A **random, per-install identifier** (not tied to your name, email, Apple ID,
  or contacts) is used only to route your match. No account is created.
- Your guesses are **not linked to your identity** and are **never used for
  tracking or advertising**.
- The server keeps only what a live match needs while it is in progress; it does
  not build a profile of you.

If you never play a live mode, nothing you type ever leaves your device. Only the anonymous play counts above do.

## Your data

If you delete the app, all of its on-device data is removed with it. Because we
hold no account and no persistent profile, there is no stored personal data to
request, export, or delete from us.
