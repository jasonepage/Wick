Wick Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Private by design, in plain language.

Wick is built to be private by design. There are no accounts, no logins, no advertising, and no cross-app tracking. Most of the game never touches a server at all. The only thing we count is a handful of anonymous play events, described below.

On-device — Single-player (Daily & Practice)

  • Your guesses, questions, stats, streaks, and coins are stored only on your device.
  • Closeness (“warmth”) scoring uses Apple’s on-device Natural Language models.
  • “Ask the Keeper” runs on-device using Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models).
  • Nothing you type in single-player is sent to us or any third party.

The one exception — Live Race

Live multiplayer needs a server to pair players and score fairly, so when — and only when — you play a live Race:

  • The words and questions you type are sent to Wick’s server to be scored (warmth) and answered (the Keeper).
  • To compute those, the server passes that text to Google’s Gemini API. It is used solely to score the round; it is not used to train models on your identity, and it is not sold.
  • A random, per-install identifier (not tied to your name, email, Apple ID, or contacts) is used only to route your match. No account is created.
  • Your guesses are not linked to your identity and are never used for tracking or advertising.
  • The server keeps only what a live match needs while it is in progress; it does not build a profile of you.

If you never play a live mode, nothing you type ever leaves your device.

Play counts (since version 3.3)

To know whether the game is working, the app and the website send a few anonymous events: that a daily puzzle was started, solved, or given up (with the puzzle number and how many guesses it took), and that a share or race link was used. Each event carries a random per-install id so we can count players; the server hashes that id before storing it, and it is not tied to your name, email, Apple ID, or contacts. No word, question, or anything you type is ever included. These counts are used only to improve the game and are not shared or sold.

Your data

If you delete the app, all of its on-device data is removed with it. Because we hold no account and no persistent profile, there is no stored personal data to request, export, or delete from us.
