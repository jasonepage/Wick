Three steps, one word

No letter clues. No green-and-yellow tiles. Just meaning.

1

Guess anything

Type any word at all. Wick scores what it means against the hidden answer — there are no spelling hints to lean on.

2

Read the warmth

Each guess lands on a scale from freezing to blazing. Closer in meaning burns hotter, so the trail tells you where to look next.

3

Home in & solve

Chase the heat, ask the Keeper yes/no questions, and close in on the exact word before your streak cools.

Private by design

Wick was built to collect as little as possible. Your single-player game never leaves your phone — and live modes only ever handle a match while it’s happening.

Read the privacy policy →
  • ✓No accounts, no logins. Nothing to sign up for.
  • ✓No ads, no tracking. You’re never the product.
  • ✓Single-player stays on-device. Guesses, stats and streaks live only on your phone.
  • ✓Live play is minimal. Only a live Race sends what you type — never tied to your identity.
  • ✓Open source. The app, the web client and the server are all on GitHub, so you can check every claim above.
