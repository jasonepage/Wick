How do I play?

There’s one secret word each day. Type any word and Wick shows how close it is in meaning — freezing (blue) through blazing (red). Follow the heat and keep guessing to home in.

What is “Ask the Keeper”?

An AI that answers yes/no questions about the secret word without revealing it. In single-player it runs on-device and needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on; the rest of the game works on any device.

How does racing work?

There are two ways. Race a friend: finish a puzzle, tap Race a friend, and send the link. Your friend plays the same word against a replay of your run, and they can play it in a browser with no install. Race someone live: Wick pairs you with another player right now and you both chase the same word. First to solve wins. No account is needed for either. (Dare was retired in version 3.3.)

Does it work offline?

Yes — the daily puzzle, warmth scoring, and hints all run on your device with no internet. Only live Race, race links, and buying coins need a connection.

What are coins for?

Optional extra hints and questions. The daily puzzle is always fully playable for free.

Can I play in a browser?

Yes — you can jump straight into a game on the web with no install, or download the iPhone app for the full experience.

How is my privacy handled?

Single-player stays entirely on your device. Live Race sends the words you type to our server (and Google’s Gemini) only to score the round — never linked to your identity. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

Still need help?

Email support@guesswick.com and we’ll get back to you. Telling us your device and iOS version helps us sort it out faster.
