# Wick 3.8 share-card art direction

![Share-card visual plate](./wick-share-card-visual-plate-v1.png)

The image above is a visual plate, not a finished screenshot. Claude should render the typography and live result data in code so the card stays crisp and accurate.

## Exact copy hierarchy

```text
WICK DAILY #248
7 GUESSES

[actual heat trail]

Can you turn Wick green?
guesswick.com
```

Replace the example day and guess count with real values. The secret word remains hidden unless the player deliberately chooses a spoiler version.

## Color roles

- Background: midnight violet / nearly black.
- Solved character: emerald green.
- Main number: warm white.
- Brand accent and rings: violet.
- Small sparks: gold.
- Heat trail: actual blue-to-teal-to-amber-to-red-to-green progression.

Purple is the world and brand. Green is the earned solved state.

## Composition

- Square canvas.
- Copy block occupies the left 44%.
- Wick occupies the right 48%.
- Keep an 8% outer safe margin.
- Wick is head only.
- The heat trail should read at thumbnail size.
- Avoid covering Wick's eyes or brows with interface elements.
